description: Automated Assemblr builds catalogue pages in InDesign from an Excel brief and your folders of images, and keeps the pages and the brief in sync both ways.
price: $99 a month

# highlights

## Images matched to the brief
Each image finds its row in the brief by the product code in its file or folder name, or by the product name. A near match is still placed, and marked Check.

## Placed and cropped
Each matched image goes into its product's cell, cropped, and is arranged with that product's other images.

## Drag in to add
Drag an image into a cell and it joins that product, arranged with the rest. There's no placing, fitting or lining up by hand.

## Free pages
Switch a page from Managed to Free and you can make image areas any size. Their images and text stay linked to the product, and in sync with the brief.

## Reorder products
Swap two products or change the whole order, and the pages reflow to match in one undo step.
