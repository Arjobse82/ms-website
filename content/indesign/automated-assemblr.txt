# highlights

## Smart matching.
Each image file finds its row in the spreadsheet by the product code in its file or folder name, or by the product's name. A near match is placed and marked Check.

## Images auto-place with cropping.
Each matched file goes into its product's cell, auto-cropped, and arranges itself there with the product's other images.

## Drop in, arranged.
Drag an image into a cell and it joins that product, arranged with the rest. Nothing to place, fit or line up by hand.

## Freestyle pages.
Set a page to Freestyle and make any image area any size. Its images and text stay linked to their product, and in step with the brief.

## Reorder in one move.
Swap two products or change the whole order, and the plugin re-flows the pages to follow, in one undo step.
