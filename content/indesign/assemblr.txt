name: Assemblr
tagline: Product layouts in InDesign, from one frame.
description: Assemblr arranges pack shots inside a single frame on your InDesign page. Pick an arrangement, put a badge on any corner, then resize the frame and Assemblr lays everything out again to fit.

# highlights

## Aligned on the product
Assemblr reads the transparency in each image and lines products up by their visible edges, so empty canvas around a pack shot doesn't throw out the spacing.

## One frame controls the layout
Click Assemble and the images go on a layer of their own, inside an empty rectangle. Resize the rectangle, even from landscape to portrait, and Assemblr lays the images out again.

## Five arrangements
Line-up, Grid, Mosaic, Pins and Puzzle. Each one remembers its own settings, so you can switch and come back without starting over.

## Corner badges
Put a badge on any corner: a logo, a graphic or a price device. Price devices use your own InDesign design: type Xs where the digits go and Assemblr fills them in, keeping your styles. Each badge holds its distance from the edges as the layout changes.

## Hand edits are kept
Crop, scale or rotate an image yourself. Assemblr waits until you deselect, then updates the layout once. Your crop is always kept; scale and rotation are kept in Line-up, Grid and Puzzle.

# specs

Works with: InDesign 2026 (21.0 or later)
Arrangements: Line-up, Grid, Mosaic, Pins and Puzzle
Undo: Every action is one undo step
Platform: macOS. No account, no internet connection needed
