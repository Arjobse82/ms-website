name: Assemblr
tagline: A page layout engine.
description: A page layout engine that can arrange images into various formations with the ability to add overlay graphics or price devices to each corner. Everything automatically resizes and readjusts using a single bounding rectangle. 

# highlights

## Lined up by what shows.
Each image is arranged by its visible subject, read from the file's transparency, so the products line up, not just their frames.

## One rectangle drives it all.
Assemble puts the images on a layer of their own and draws an empty rectangle round them. Move it, or drag it from landscape to portrait, and they are laid out again for the new shape.

## Five arrangements.
Line-up, Grid, Mosaic, Pins or Puzzle, each keeping its own settings for when you switch back. Change a setting and the page follows.

## Badges and prices on the corners.
Load a badge onto any corner, or a price in your own InDesign design: the digits go where its Xs are, its styles kept. Each keeps its distance from the edges as the product changes shape.

## Your edits, kept.
Crop, scale or turn an image by hand. Assemblr waits until you deselect, then lays the product out once: the crop is kept, and the scale and turn in Line-up, Grid and Puzzle.

# specs

Works with: InDesign 2026 (21.0 or later)
Arrangements: Line-up, Grid, Mosaic, Pins and Puzzle
Undo: One step for every action
Platform: macOS, no account, no network
Availability: Adobe Exchange
