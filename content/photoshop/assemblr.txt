name: Assemblr
tagline: Pack shot layouts in Photoshop.
description: Assemblr arranges pack shots on their layers inside an invisible rectangle. Pick one of five arrangements, resize the rectangle and the layers are fitted again. Every layer stays editable.

# highlights

## Aligned on the product
Assemblr lines products up by their visible pixels, measured from each layer's transparency, so empty space in the layer doesn't affect the spacing.

## Five arrangements
Line-up, Grid, Mosaic, Pins and Puzzle, inside an invisible rectangle. Resize the rectangle and the layers are fitted again.

## Hand edits are kept
Scale a layer yourself and Assemblr keeps its size. Lock a layer and it stays put while the others fit around it.

## One history step per action
Every action is a single named history step, and the layout is saved in the document.
