HOW THE SITE SHOULD SOUND
=========================

The writing guide is shared by every Monochrome Systems project (this site,
the plugins' panels and help pages), so it lives in one place:

    ~/.claude/skills/trade-copy/SKILL.md

Short version: write like a plugin maker or a senior artworker on the
InDesign forum. Use plain sentences, exact UI names and real numbers. No
slogans, no "not X but Y", no groups of three.
