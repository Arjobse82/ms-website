# highlights

## Works on copies
Ad Agent never edits your working files. It works on copies.

## InDesign settings per channel
Each channel can run its own InDesign Preflight profile and PDF preset, including a printer's .joboptions file.

## Tags on every size
Each size is tagged with its creative, channel and tool as it's made. Add your own tags, like Retouching or Creative review, and filter the job by any of them.

## Files routed by tag
Tag a size Approved and Ad Agent makes its high-res PDF with your High res preset, then sends it to the folder you chose.

label: Safety
title: What it can and can't touch
intro: Ad Agent works on copies, asks macOS once before it controls InDesign, and its AI changes only what you allow. This is what it can reach.

## The AI
You set its reach: Turn each ability on or off, or pick one of three levels: Look only, Default or Full control.
Nothing changes unasked: The AI does what you ask. Anything it suggests on its own waits for your approval.
Before and after: Every change to copy or layout shows its before and after first.
Client documents: Once the AI has read an email or a document, every change it proposes waits for your approval.
No runs on its own: The AI can't remake sizes until you allow it.
What it cannot do: Run InDesign scripts, edit your brief's spreadsheet, or pick the winner of an A/B.

## Your files
Originals protected: Ad Agent never opens or edits your originals. It works on copies.
Nothing deleted: A replaced file moves to its round's Archive folder. Nothing in a round is deleted.
Past rounds kept: Earlier rounds are read-only, kept as delivered.
Its own files only: A send touches only the job's own files, and two jobs can never write to the same folder.
Shared jobs: One Mac edits a job at a time. Everyone else opens it read-only.
InDesign as you left it: Your PDF export settings are put back after every job.

## Folder access
Where it writes: New sizes go in an Ad Agent sizes folder next to your masters, with a folder for each round.
Folders you choose: Files are sent only to the folders you add to the job, and each one has its own on/off switch.
Checked when chosen: When you add a folder, Ad Agent checks it can save there, so you find out straight away rather than at despatch.
macOS asks first: The first time Ad Agent reaches a protected place, like Documents, Desktop or a server, macOS asks you once.
What the AI can open: Only files you give it, and files in the job folder, the masters folder or the Notes folder.
In Figma: Ad Agent writes only to its own page, Ad Agent sizes, and changes only the frames it made.

## Your Mac
One permission: macOS asks once before Ad Agent controls InDesign: "Ad Agent opens your masters and the sizes it writes in InDesign, to measure their text and export each size."
Yours to take back: Turn it off at any time in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Automation.
Nothing added to InDesign: No plugins or scripts are installed in it.
Figma by your choice: Figma works through Ad Agent Bridge, a plugin you add to Figma yourself.
Works offline: Making and previewing sizes needs no internet. Only releasing a size goes online.

## Your data
Your key, in your Keychain: A Claude API key is kept in the macOS Keychain and sent only to Anthropic.
Local models stay local: With a model on your Mac, nothing leaves it.
Images on request: The AI asks before sending more than four images in one request.
Text only on Standard AI: On the Standard AI plan, no images are sent at all.

# features

title: Features

## Brief and specs
Spreadsheet briefs: Excel, CSV, TSV or text, from any sheet in the workbook.
Print and screen units: px for screen; mm, cm or inches for print.
Scale, bleed and safe area: Typed in with a size, or taken from its spec.
Media specs book: Each size is matched to its format in the specs book, never guessed.
Brief updates: Pull in a new version of the brief and apply only what changed.
Brief columns as tags: Any column, like Market, can become a tag.

## Masters
InDesign masters: Open .indd or .idml. Ad Agent only ever works on copies.
Figma masters: Linked through the Ad Agent Bridge plugin.
Nothing to install in InDesign: Ad Agent drives it directly.
Your originals: Drop in finished files and they join the job.
Books and multi-page files: Every page is delivered.
Colour: RGB, CMYK, spot and Lab are read, including in linked artwork.

## Rollout engine
Every size laid out: Each size is built from the master closest to its shape. Elements are moved and scaled, never distorted.
An .indd for every size: Edits you make in it are kept when the size is made again.
Component settings: Pin, clamp, stretch, cover and collision, set layer by layer.
Logo profiles: The logo goes in a set corner at a set size, per channel or per size.
Copy changes: Change the words on a master, a channel or a single size, with a before-and-after check.
File naming: Built from the channel, job number, creative, size or any brief column.

## Rounds and review
Rounds: Each round has its own folder, and past rounds are kept as delivered.
Live rounds: Close a file in InDesign and its exports are made again.
Compare rounds: Rounds stack like layers, with a list of what changed.
Change lists: Each round writes down what changed.
A/B variants: Make a B version of any size, then retire one or split it out.
Archive: Replaced files move to the round's archive. Nothing is deleted.

## Tags and workflows
Tag filters: Show or hide sizes by tag. Filters never change what gets made.
System, user and AI tags: Each kind on its own row.
Tag tools: Colour, rename, merge and add notes to tags, or select everything with a tag.
Routing by tag: Tags decide which files each size gets and where they go.
Folders as tags: A folder's subfolders become tags.
Job files: Each job autosaves. One Mac edits it at a time; others can open it read-only.

## Preflight and quality
Layout checks: Elements off the page, collisions, small legal text and elements over the safe area.
Image resolution: Estimated ppi for every size, print and screen.
Masters check: Fonts, RGB, overprint, thin strokes, spelling and missing links.
InDesign Preflight: Your chosen profile runs on every size in a channel.
One preflight list: Findings from InDesign, Ad Agent and the AI in one list.
Held back: Sizes with problems can be kept out of a folder.

## Output and delivery
PDF, JPG and PNG: PDF from an InDesign preset; JPG with a quality setting and a KB limit.
Printer's presets: Load a .joboptions file.
Approval PDFs: A channel's sizes on one sheet, or one size per page.
Figma exports: JPG, PNG or PDF from sizes built in Figma.
Destination folders: Local or on a server, each with its own send switch.
Colour sends: RGB and CMYK files go to their own folders.

## System
Mac app: macOS 13 or later, light and dark mode.
InDesign: Needed for .indd masters and exports. Without it, IDML only.
Figma: The desktop app with the Ad Agent Bridge plugin.
Works offline: Only releasing a size needs the internet.
Low-res previews: Free. The editable files and final exports come when you release a size.
