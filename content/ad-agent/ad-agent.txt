label: Safety
title: You stay in control.
intro: Ad Agent works on copies, asks macOS once before it touches InDesign, and its AI changes only what you allow. Here is what it can reach, and what it cannot.

## The AI
You set its reach: Each of its abilities on or off, or one of three settings: Look only, Default or Full control.
Nothing changes unasked: What you ask for is done. What the AI suggests on its own waits for your approval.
Before and after: Every change to copy or layout is shown before and after first.
Client documents: Once the AI has read an email or a document, every change it proposes waits for your approval.
No runs on its own: Making sizes again is off until you allow it.
What it cannot do: Run InDesign scripts, edit your brief's spreadsheet, or pick the winner of an A/B.

## Your files
Masters untouched: Ad Agent never opens or edits your masters. It works on copies.
Nothing deleted: A replaced file moves to its round's Archive folder. Nothing in a round is deleted.
Past rounds kept: Earlier rounds are look only, kept as delivered.
Its own files only: A send touches only the job's own files, and two jobs can never write to the same folder.
Shared jobs: One Mac edits a job at a time. Everyone else opens it read-only.
InDesign as you left it: Your PDF export settings are put back after every job.

## Folder access
Where it writes: New sizes go in an Ad Agent sizes folder beside your masters, a folder for each round.
Folders you choose: Files are sent only to the folders you add to the job, each with its own on and off switch.
Checked when chosen: When you add a folder, Ad Agent checks it can save there, so a problem shows then, not at despatch.
macOS asks first: The first time Ad Agent reaches a protected place, like Documents, Desktop or a server, macOS asks you once.
What the AI can open: Only a file you give it, or one in the job's, the masters' or the Notes folder.
In Figma: Ad Agent writes only to its own page, Ad Agent sizes, and changes only the frames it made.

## Your Mac
One permission: macOS asks once before Ad Agent controls InDesign: "Ad Agent opens your masters and the sizes it writes in InDesign, to measure their text and export each size."
Yours to take back: Turn it off at any time in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Automation.
Nothing added to InDesign: No plugins or scripts are installed in it.
Figma by your choice: Figma works through Ad Agent Bridge, a plugin you add to Figma yourself.
Works offline: Making and previewing sizes needs no internet. Only releasing a size goes online.

## Your data
Your key, in your Keychain: A Claude API key is kept in the macOS Keychain and sent only to Anthropic.
Local models stay local: With a model on your Mac, nothing leaves it.
Pictures on request: The AI asks before sending more than four pictures in one question.
Text only on Standard AI: On the Standard AI plan, no pictures are sent at all.

# features
// The full feature list under the specs. A group a "##"; a feature a line under it, "Name: what it does".

title: Features

## Brief and specs
Spreadsheet briefs: Excel, CSV, TSV or text, any sheet of the book.
Print and screen units: px for screens; mm, cm or inches for print.
Scale, bleed and safe area: Typed with a size, or taken from its spec.
Media specs book: Each size matched to its format, never guessed.
Brief updates: Pull a new version of the brief and apply only what changed.
Brief columns as tags: Any column, like Market, becomes a tag.

## Masters
InDesign masters: Open .indd or .idml. Ad Agent only ever works on copies.
Figma masters: Linked through the Ad Agent Bridge plugin.
Nothing to install in InDesign: Ad Agent drives it directly.
Your own files: Drop in finished files and they join the job.
Books and multi-page files: Every page delivered.
Colour read: RGB, CMYK, spot and LAB, linked artwork included.

## Rollout engine
Every size laid out: From the master nearest its shape, moved and scaled, not squashed.
An .indd for every size: Edits made in it are kept when it is made again.
Component settings: Pin, clamp, stretch, cover and collision, layer by layer.
Logo profiles: The logo in a set corner at a set size, per channel or size.
Copy changes: Change the words on a master, a channel or a size, checked before and after.
File naming: Built from the channel, job number, creative, size or any brief column.

## Rounds and review
Rounds: Each round in its own folder, the past ones kept as delivered.
Live rounds: Close a file in InDesign and its exports are made again.
Compare rounds: Rounds stacked like layers, with a list of what changed.
Change lists: Each round writes down what changed.
A/B variants: Make a B of any size, then retire one or split it out.
Archive: Replaced files move to the round's archive. Nothing is deleted.

## Tags and workflows
Tag filters: Show or hide by tag; filters never change what gets made.
System, user and AI tags: Each on its own row.
Tag tools: Colour, rename, merge, note, and select everything with a tag.
Routing by tag: Tags decide which files each size gets and where they go.
Folders as tags: A folder's subfolders become tags.
Job files: Each job autosaves. One Mac edits it at a time; others can read it.

## Preflight and quality
Layout checks: Off-page elements, collisions, small legal text and crossed safe areas.
Image resolution: Estimated ppi for every size, print and screen.
Masters check: Fonts, RGB, overprint, thin strokes, spelling and missing links.
InDesign Preflight: Your chosen profile runs on every size of a channel.
One preflight list: InDesign's, Ad Agent's and the AI's findings together.
Held back: Sizes with problems can be kept out of a folder.

## Output and delivery
PDF, JPG and PNG: PDF by InDesign preset; JPG with quality and a KB cap.
Printer's presets: Load a .joboptions file.
Approval PDFs: A channel's sizes joined in one PDF, or a sheet a size a page.
Figma exports: JPG, PNG or PDF from Figma-built sizes.
Destination folders: Local or on a server, each with its own send switch.
Colour sends: RGB and CMYK files to their own folders.

## System
Mac app: macOS 13 or later, light and dark.
InDesign: Needed for .indd masters and exports. Without it, IDML only.
Figma: The desktop app with the Ad Agent Bridge plugin.
Works offline: Only releasing a size needs the internet.
Low-res previews: Free. Working files and exports come with release.
