## Brand colours to CMYK
Brand colours in RGB are mapped to CMYK swatches and saved in a client profile. The next job picks them up by name.

## Overprint for InDesign
Set overprint on the fills or strokes of shapes and text in Figma, and it carries across to InDesign.

## Hi-res links
Connect a folder on your Mac. Images whose layer names match a JPEG, PNG, PSD, AI, EPS or PDF in it link to that file instead of Figma's copy.
