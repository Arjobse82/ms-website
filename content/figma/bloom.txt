# tour

bloom-tour-1: Bloom: a rosette of fine lines with error markers round it, reading 65 PPI, low resolution.
bloom-tour-2: Preflight, error detection, token fixes and quality control, beside two of Bloom's panels.
bloom-tour-3: Find and fix: off-pixel grid, stretched components, low-res images, unlocked aspect ratios, raw colours, raw corner radii and raw text. Safety detection: hidden layers, loose layers, detached instances, placeholder text and file dimension errors.
bloom-tour-4: Each error populates around the bloom: a data visualisation that overlays all your frames as a unique rosette.
bloom-tour-5: A low-resolution image at 59 dpi: upload the full-resolution file, or bring it up to 72, 144 or 300 dpi as a new layer or variant.
bloom-tour-6: A raw corner radius of 25: add it to your local library, or pick an existing variable from your library or design system.
