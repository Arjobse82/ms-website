name: Bloom
tagline: Preflight for Figma frames.
description: Bloom checks your Figma frames for low-res images, off-pixel positions, detached instances, raw colours and other errors, and offers a fix where it can.
price: US$4 a month

# tour

bloom-tour-1: Bloom's rosette: a drawing of fine lines with error markers round it, here flagging a low-resolution image at 65 PPI.
bloom-tour-2: Two of Bloom's panels, with what it covers: preflight, error detection, token fixes and quality control.
bloom-tour-3: What Bloom finds and fixes: off-pixel positions, stretched components, low-res images, unlocked aspect ratios, and raw colours, corner radii and text. It also flags hidden layers, loose layers, detached instances, placeholder text and wrong frame sizes.
bloom-tour-4: Each error is plotted round the rosette, which overlays all your frames in one drawing.
bloom-tour-5: A low-resolution image at 59 dpi. Upload the full-resolution file, or upscale it to 72, 144 or 300 dpi as a new layer or variant.
bloom-tour-6: A raw corner radius of 25. Add it to your local library, or swap it for a variable from your library or design system.
