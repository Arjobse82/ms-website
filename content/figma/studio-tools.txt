name: Studio Tools
tagline: All our Figma plugins in one.
description: Bloom, Frame Multiplier and IDML Export, plus seven smaller widgets, in one Figma plugin.

# highlights

## Logo checks on every frame
Studio Tools measures each frame's logo against the brand rules: its size, clear space and corner, for every channel and size. Double-click to fix it on one frame or across the whole chart.

## One dashboard
Bloom, Frame Multiplier and IDML Export each sit on the dashboard as a tile, alongside seven widgets.

## Page summary
When the plugin opens it lists the page's creatives and channels, and what's measured in each frame.

## Synced with your folders
Images, exports and the Excel brief stay in sync with your folders through Monochrome Bridge.
