# principles

## Native
Works like the app it runs in
Each tool follows the conventions of its host app, so there's very little new to learn.

## Scale
Written for big jobs
Large files, long catalogues and big batches are the normal case these tools are built for.

## Predictable
The same result every time
Run it again on the same files and you get the same layout, so anyone on the team can repeat the job.

## Quiet
Stays out of the way
The panel shows the settings for the job in hand and nothing else.
